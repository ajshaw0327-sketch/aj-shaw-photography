The photographer

A patient eye for lived-in moments.

I'm AJ Shaw, a Massachusetts-based photographer drawn to honest color, quick gestures, and the small visual clues that make a place or gathering feel real.

Working with a Fujifilm X-T50, I approach travel, events, and sports with the same documentary instinct: stay curious, pay attention, and let the photograph feel like the moment did.
